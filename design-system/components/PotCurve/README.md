# PotCurve

The bowl of the logo's pot, stretched into a full-width edge. Place it directly after a coloured band so the band ends in a curve instead of a straight line.

- Provide: `color`, matching the band above (`brown`, `surface`, `sunken` or `orange`); `flip` to lead into a band instead.
- The section after it keeps its own background; the curve sits on it.
- Use it at most twice per page (after the hero, before the footer). Every edge curving makes none of them special.
- Related classes from the same logo: `.su-hook` (the logo's top hook stroke as a short rule) and `.su-hook-under` (the hook as an underline for one or two words of a headline). `.su-pot` and `.su-pot-lg` give any box the pot's neck-and-belly corners.
